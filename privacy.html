Privacy Policy – FieldCam

Effective Date: 2025

1. Introduction

FieldCam is a field photography and documentation application designed to help users capture photos and videos with visible time, location, and related metadata for documentation and record-keeping purposes.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how users remain in control of their data. FieldCam operates primarily on the user’s device and does not use developer-controlled servers.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Photos & Videos

  • Photos and videos captured using the FieldCam application
  • Optional original (unstamped) media if enabled by the user

Purpose: To capture, display, and manage visual records within the app.

2.2 Location Information

  • GPS latitude and longitude
  • Location accuracy
  • Altitude (if available)

Location data is collected only at the time of photo or video capture. FieldCam does not collect location data in the background and does not perform continuous location tracking.

2.3 User-Provided Information

  • Notes, labels, or categories entered by the user
  • Optional name or identifier entered by the user

2.4 Capture Metadata

  • Date and time of capture
  • Time source (device, network, or GPS)
  • Time zone at capture location

3. How We Use Information

  • Adding visible metadata to photos and videos
  • Generating QR codes that reference captured information
  • Organizing records in gallery and location-based views
  • Exporting files in user-requested formats such as PDF, CSV, ZIP, or KML/KMZ

FieldCam does not use user data for advertising, profiling, or resale.

4. Data Storage & Retention

4.1 On-Device Storage

All photos, videos, and related metadata are stored locally on the user’s device by default. Users retain full control over their data and may delete it at any time.

4.2 Optional User-Initiated Export

Users may manually export or share their files using methods they choose. FieldCam does not automatically transmit data and does not maintain any developer-controlled servers.

5. Data Sharing

  • FieldCam does not sell user data
  • FieldCam does not share data with advertisers
  • No data is transmitted to developer-owned servers

6. App Permissions & Usage

  • Camera access is used only to capture photos and videos
  • Location access is used only while capturing media to attach location information
  • No permissions are used in the background

7. User Control

  • Grant or revoke permissions at any time
  • Enable or disable metadata and stamping features
  • Control file export and sharing
  • Delete stored data directly from the device

8. Children’s Privacy

FieldCam is not intended for children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect feature changes or legal requirements. Updated versions will be made available within the app or on the official website.
