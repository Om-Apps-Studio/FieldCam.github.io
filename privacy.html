Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 19, 2025

Core Principles

  • No Data Selling: We never sell or share your personal data.
  • No Ads: FieldCam is 100% ad-free.
  • No Trackers: We do not track your behavior inside the app.
  • User Ownership: You own 100% of your photos, videos, and metadata.

Permissions Explanation

FieldCam requires certain permissions to function as a professional documentation tool:

  • Camera: Used solely to capture photos and videos within the app.
  • Location (GPS): Used to embed precise coordinates into your media stamps for verification.
  • Storage/Media: Used to save your captured files to your device's gallery and access them for reports.
  • Microphone: Used only for recording audio during video capture.

Data Storage

All media captured with FieldCam is stored locally on your device. We do not use developer-side servers to store your photos or videos. If you choose to use cloud sync features, your data is sent only to your own configured cloud storage provider (e.g., Google Drive) and is not accessible to us.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@fieldcam.app.
