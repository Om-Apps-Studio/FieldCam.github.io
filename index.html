What is FieldCam?

FieldCam is a professional field documentation camera designed for inspectors, engineers, surveyors, government staff, and documentation professionals. It embeds date, time, GPS, notes, and verification data directly into photos and videos, ensuring authenticity and long-term trust.

Verified Stamping

Photo & Video stamping with date, time, and precise GPS location burned into the media.

Secure QR Verification

Encrypted QR codes for instant verification of media authenticity, even after sharing.

Tamper Detection

Industry-standard SHA-256 hashing to detect if any pixel has been altered.

Offline-First

Full functionality without internet. Perfect for remote sites and high-security areas.

Professional Exports

Generate PDF reports, KML files for Google Earth, and ZIP archives with ease.

WhatsApp-Safe

Verification data survives compression, keeping your evidence valid across all apps.
