Security
Does FieldCam store my photos on its servers?
No. FieldCam is an offline-first app. Your photos and videos are stored locally on your device in the Pictures/FieldCam and Movies/FieldCam folders. We have no servers to store your media.
Dec 15, 2025
General
Can I use FieldCam without a GPS signal?
Yes, you can still capture photos. The app will use your last known location or device time. However, the GPS verification badge will indicate that coordinates are missing.
Dec 12, 2025

Is the PDF report customizable?
Currently, you can choose which photos to include and add a project name. We are working on adding custom logos and header information for organizational reports in the next update.
