Simple, Secure Workflow

01

Capture

Open FieldCam and capture a photo or video. All necessary permissions (Camera, Location) are handled securely.

02

Stamp & Verify

The app automatically calculates a SHA-256 hash and generates an encrypted QR code. Metadata is visually stamped and embedded into the file header.

03

Store & Organize

Files are saved locally in structured folders (Pictures/FieldCam/YYYY-MM/Category). No data ever leaves your device unless you share it.

04

Verify Anytime

Anyone with the photo can scan the embedded QR code using FieldCam or a standard scanner to verify its date, time, location, and authenticity.

05

Share or Export

Send photos directly via WhatsApp or email, or export comprehensive PDF/KML reports for your clients or management.
